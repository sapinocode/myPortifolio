Participant National Innovation Competition

MAKISATU — National Science, Technology & Innovation Competition

COSTECH Ministry of Education Tanga

Participated in the Mashindano ya Kitaifa ya Sayansi, Teknolojia na Ubunifu (MAKISATU) — Tanzania's national science, technology and innovation competition. The event is organised by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology through the Tanzania Commission for Science and Technology (COSTECH), with the aim of developing and providing technical and financial support to innovators across the country.

MAKISATU brought together innovators, researchers, and technology enthusiasts from across Tanzania — a very different environment from an academic conference. It exposed me to practical problem-solving, the discipline of turning research into a working product, and the realities of pitching ideas to panels that judge on impact, feasibility, and scalability.

Interacting with different people and ideas strengthened my appreciation for the connection between technology, innovation, research, and entrepreneurship — and showed me what it takes to move an idea from a notebook to something a funder or partner can back.

Why it mattered Meeting innovators supported by COSTECH and the Ministry helped me see a clear path from technical skill to national-scale impact — and motivated me to keep building software, data, and AI work that solves real problems.
